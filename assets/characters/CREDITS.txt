At runtime (index.html) the game, without changing the files:
  - freezes each model on its "Idle_Neutral" animation clip and bends the leg,
    head and arm bones to pose it seated at a desk and to animate "tells";
  - applies scale, rotation and position offsets to place it in the classroom;
  - adds simple props (phone, notes, paper plane, snack) as separate meshes;
  - ONLY IF an optional file assets/face.glb is present locally (it is not
    shipped in this repository), hides the model's face-plate meshes (materials
    "Skin", "Skin_Darker", "Eyebrows", "Eye" in the head group) and attaches a
    separate expressive head in their place.


WHAT THE FILES THEMSELVES SAY
-----------------------------

Checked on 2026-09-24 by reading each file's glTF JSON header: every file has
asset.generator "FBX2glTF v0.9.7" and asset.version "2.0", and no copyright,
author, licence or "extras" metadata. The files therefore do not name their
creator or licence; the information above rests on the download record.
