Modifications: geometry and rig are unchanged. For web delivery the files were re-packed with
scripts/optimize-assets.mjs: animation clips the game never plays were removed (only
"Idle_Neutral" is kept, plus "Walk" for business-man.glb), and the geometry was
meshopt-compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression) with normals, UVs and skin weights quantized.
