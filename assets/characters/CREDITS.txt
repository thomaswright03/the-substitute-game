CHANGES
-------

Stored files. For web delivery the files were re-packed with scripts/optimize-assets.mjs:
animation clips the game never plays were removed (only "Idle_Neutral" is kept, plus "Walk"
for business-man.glb), and the geometry was meshopt-compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression)
with normals, UVs and skin weights quantized. The original rig and mesh shapes are
otherwise unchanged. SHA-256 of each file as committed:

At runtime the game, without further changing the files:
  - freezes each model on its "Idle_Neutral" animation clip and bends the leg, head and
    arm bones to pose it seated at a desk and to animate "tells";
  - applies scale, rotation and position offsets to place it in the classroom;
  - adds simple props (phone, notes, paper plane, snack) as separate meshes;
  - gives each head an expressive face (src/face.js): blend shapes added to the head's own
    eye and brow meshes, and a generated mouth. No third-party face model is used.


WHAT THE ORIGINAL FILES SAID
----------------------------

Checked on 2026-09-24 by reading each as-downloaded file's glTF JSON header: every file had
asset.generator "FBX2glTF v0.9.7" and asset.version "2.0", and no copyright, author,
licence or "extras" metadata. The files therefore did not name their creator or licence;
the information above rests on the download record.
