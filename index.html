First Day Sub Plans

The Substitute

First: take attendance. Eight name cards sit pinned to the chalkboard — pick one up, then find that student and hand it over to mark them present. Eight trips to the board, and your back is turned every time. After that, walk the room. Get close to a student who's acting up and step in before it escalates. Someone might throw something whenever you're facing the board — turn around fast enough and you've caught them. No one leaves the room, no one gets hurt.

About This Game

Credits & Privacy

Your data

The game has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it makes no requests to any third party. Every script, model and font is a local file in this project, so your browser only fetches the game’s own files from the server you loaded the page from (for example your own python3 -m http.server).

It stores one thing, in your browser’s localStorage on this device: your best survival time, under the key substitute_best. It is never sent anywhere.

Content note

All characters are fictional. The game uses crude, innuendo-style pun names for students and a cartoon “lightning zap” discipline option. It is intended for adults and is not designed for or directed at children.
